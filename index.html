About me

I am currently a Ph.D. scholar in the Department of Mathematics at IIT Jammu, working under the supervision of Dr. Rajiv Kumar.

My doctoral work has led me to explore several problems at the interface of algebra and combinatorics, and I am particularly interested in developing these directions further through new collaborations.

My doctoral research lies at the intersection of commutative algebra and combinatorics, with a particular focus on monomial ideals and their associated combinatorial structures. I am also interested in broader questions involving homological methods in commutative algebra and algebraic combinatorics, as well as emerging interests in combinatorial topology and the connections between combinatorics and geometry.

Research

My current research focuses on the following themes:

Monomial ideals

Structural and homological properties of monomial ideals and their combinatorial interpretations.

Symbolic powers

Symbolic powers, ordinary powers, containments, and related algebraic properties.

Edge ideals

Edge ideals, generalized edge ideals, weighted structures, and associated combinatorial questions.

Associated primes

Associated primes, persistence phenomena, and their relationship with combinatorial structures.
